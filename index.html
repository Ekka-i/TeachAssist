    Frequently Asked Questions

    Is TeachAssist writing my work for me?

    No. TeachAssist scaffolds the thinking: it suggests structure, asks pedagogical questions, and checks alignment. You decide what goes into the final material.

    How do I export a material to PDF?

    Open the workspace, then use the print icon in the editor toolbar. Choose "Save as PDF" as the destination.

    Can I share a material with my cooperating teacher?

    Yes. Open the material and press Share in the top bar. You can grant "Can view" or "Can edit" access. Shared items appear under Shared with Me for the recipient.

    What happens when I delete something?

    It moves to the Recycle Bin and stays there for 30 days. You can restore it at any point during that window, or delete it forever immediately.

    Which grade levels are supported?

    All of them. Set the grade level on any material and the coach adjusts vocabulary, complexity, and time estimates accordingly.

    Is there a free plan?

    Yes. The free plan includes all ten material builders with a monthly generation limit. Pro unlocks unlimited generations, premium templates, and rubric exports.
